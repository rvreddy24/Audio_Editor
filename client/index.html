Step-by-Step Tool User Guides

How to Merge Multiple PDFs

  1. Select the PDF & Office Studio on the homepage.
  2. Click Merge PDF and drag your PDF documents into the dropzone.
  3. Reorder the page thumbnails into your desired sequence.
  4. Click Merge PDFs to combine files locally and download immediately.

How to Compress Images to Exact KB

  1. Open Image Studio and select Target KB Compressor.
  2. Upload your JPG or PNG image (photo, signature, or document scan).
  3. Enter your target file size in kilobytes (e.g., 20KB, 50KB, or 100KB).
  4. Our binary-search algorithm scales quality in real-time. Download the exact-size image.

How to Cut & Trim Audio Waveforms

  1. Open Audio & Video Studio and drop an MP3, WAV, or video file.
  2. Use visual waveform markers to set precise start and end timestamps.
  3. Apply optional fade-in, fade-out, or volume normalization effects.
  4. Export your clean audio track at high bitrates up to 320 kbps.

How to Calculate Loan & Mortgage Amortization

  1. Open Calculators and choose Mortgage or Loan EMI.
  2. Input your principal loan amount, annual interest rate, and term duration.
  3. View monthly breakdown including principal, interest, taxes, and total interest paid.
  4. Explore the comprehensive year-by-year amortization schedule for payoff planning.
